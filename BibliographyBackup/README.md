# Bibliography provenance and edition choices

`invalid-original/` preserves the three supplied JSON files byte-for-byte before reconstruction. They are historical inputs, not app resources.

The bibliography separates first UK periodical publication year (`firstUKPublicationYear`) from the original book publication year (`publicationYear` on novels and collections). Neither field is an investigation date. The nine book positions follow the University of Minnesota Libraries' Sherlock Holmes canon overview: https://static.lib.umn.edu/pdf/holmes/inv.pdf. First UK publication years follow the Northern Ireland Sherlock Holmes Society's canon list: https://thessmayday.org.uk/stories/introducing-the-canon/ (the society page has a typographical 1891 for the start of *Hound*; the Minnesota source identifies 1901–1902).

Collection order and titles are checked against:

- Adventures: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1661/1661-h/1661-h.htm
- Memoirs: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/834/834-h/834-h.htm, omitting Cardboard Box for the selected eleven-story version.
- Return: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/108/108-h/108-h.htm
- His Last Bow: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/98/His_Last_Bow_%281917_NY%29.pdf, 1917 New York edition, eight stories in the printed order.
- Case-Book: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/69700/69700-h/69700-h.htm

The Cardboard Box is second in the selected 1917 New York *His Last Bow*. Some editions, including the linked Gutenberg *Memoirs*, place it in *Memoirs*; it is represented once. The project selects the eleven-story *Memoirs* and eight-story *His Last Bow*, per the requested structure. The first American *Memoirs* also included it, so the selected 1917 volume is not a claim that it was the first collected appearance. Titles such as *The Reigate Squires* / *The Reigate Puzzle* and *The Sign of Four* / *The Sign of the Four* have documented variants.
